## Voice and tone (p07)

TONE MIX: "60% PROFESSIONAL · 25% HUMAN · 15% ASPIRATIONAL".

| Trait | Say | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | "We'll collect by 14:00." | "We'll endeavour to facilitate…" |
| Trusted | "Owner-driven. Gauteng. Proof of delivery." | "World-class paradigm of mobility." |
| Urgent (when true) | "Rush slot available — rush rate applies." | "!!! AMAZING DEAL !!!" |
| Human | "Nico will be on the wheel." | "A resource will be allocated." |
| Premium calm | "Your vehicle. Safely delivered." | Cheap flash or dealership shout. |

Avoid: ""cheap / cheapest / budget" · corporate fog (leverage, synergy, seamless ecosystem) · "guaranteed anywhere, anytime" · emoji spam · talking competitors down." Language: "English is primary. Reply in Afrikaans when the client starts in Afrikaans — never mix languages in one formal sentence." The site matches: no exclamation marks or emoji; short declaratives split by full stops ("Collect. Drive. Hand over."); guard-rails that say what is not claimed ("Pins mark the areas we cover — not live driver locations.", site: /); South African English ("bakkies", "roadworthy"); house words "runner", "on its own wheels", "pin", "window", "run", "handover", "POD".
