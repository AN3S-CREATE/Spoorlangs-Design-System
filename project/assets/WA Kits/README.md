# WA Kits — WhatsApp customer updates

Eighteen PNGs exported 2026-10-09 from the Canva folder "WA Kits Launch Editable", after Andries Liebenberg corrected the contact details to spoorlangs.online: nine customer updates, each as a 1080 × 1920 story and a 1080 × 1080 square — thank-you, incident report, invoice reminder, follow-up, after delivery, delay exception, collection authority, cancel / reschedule, booking confirmation. Brand Book p27 lists the "WhatsApp kit" as in use. Edit in Canva, re-export at the same size, replace the file. All eighteen are fix-before-reuse; none prints any other domain or email address.

## The template

One layout with different copy: the approved Main Full lockup top-centre (matched pixel for pixel to `main-full-dark.png`; `dark-mode.png` is the same artwork) with "Driven By People. Further Together." under it; a headline in Bebas Neue caps; a dim meta line "Johannesburg on-wheels · runners only · Gauteng day-one"; six tick-box cards numbered 01–06 with `ignition-orange` numerals (sampled ≈#FF7A00) and Inter body; then the WhatsApp-kit footer strip — a 4–5 px orange rule over a #08090b bar with the flame-S, "WhatsApp +27 66 271 5887", "drive@spoorlangs.online · spoorlangs.online" and "Nico · Owner" (the invoice reminder: "Ask Nico for pay details"). No photographs, no stats, no amber or navy.

## Fixes every file needs

- **Flattened artwork** — only `thank-you-square` has live text in Canva; the other seventeen are single images, so any copy change means rebuilding the text (the folder is named "Editable").
- **Soft stories** — the stories are 1080 × 1920 files upscaled from about half size; rebuild and export at native size.
- **Logo ground and clear space** (p11–p13) — a faint grid and orange glow sit behind the lockup (on `thank-you-square` its black ground shows as a box); the tagline sits ≈10–14 px under the wordmark, inside the clear space (one S height: ≈25 px on the squares, ≈33 px on the stories).
- **Meta line contrast** (p26) — the dark-grey meta line measures ≈1.9–2.9:1 on black; set it in `titanium-silver`.
- **Contact wording** (p25) — coverage reads "Johannesburg on-wheels"; the book's block is "Johannesburg & Greater Gauteng, on wheels". The web line prints "spoorlangs.online"; the p25 block writes "https://spoorlangs.online" (the corrected Templates print it the same way — settle once).
- **Squares only** — card body ≈14 px (p26 body minimum 16 px).
- **Tints** — card fills and outlines #0C0D0F, #08090B, #1F2227 and the orange glow are off palette (the same open point as the Templates).
