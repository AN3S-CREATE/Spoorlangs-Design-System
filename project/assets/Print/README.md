# Print — stationery, signage, workwear and ID

Twenty-one PNGs exported 2026-10-09 from the Canva folder "Print" (inside "Launch Editable Extra"), after Andries Liebenberg corrected the contact details to spoorlangs.online. Brand Book p27 lists "Letterhead A4 · A5 leave-behind · Business card · Runner ID · Vehicle branding (target) · Workwear polo" as in use. Edit in Canva, re-export, replace the file. Where a piece prints contact details they are `drive@spoorlangs.online`, `spoorlangs.online`, "+27 66 271 5887" and "wa.me/27662715887" (the QR sticker decodes to https://wa.me/27662715887); none prints any other domain or email address. Verdicts: ten fix-before-reuse, eleven do-not-reuse, none ok as exported.

## Common to the set

- **Flattened and soft** — twenty of the twenty-one designs have no live text in Canva (only the full-bleed card has a text layer), so every copy fix means rebuilding the text. The letterheads, compliment slip, envelope and leave-behinds export soft, enlarged from small originals — not print-ready; rebuild with live text and export at 300 dpi before anything goes to a printer.
- **Typed wordmark** (p13 "never: redraw, retype or AI-generate the wordmark") — most sheets carry a typed "SPOORLANGS" masthead over a design label, and several marks pair the approved flame-S with a typed "SPOORLANGS" in place of an approved lockup. Use `horizontal.png` or `stacked.png` (p10), or the flame-S with `wordmark-only.png`.
- **Production notes printed as artwork** — spec labels ("A5 leave-behind · 148 × 210 mm · Launch", "80 mm", "~300 dpi"), "visual_gate=final", "Draft stationery · replace placeholders before use", guide notes. Delete them from client-facing pieces; supplier sheets may keep sizes.
- **Contact wording** (p25) — "Johannesburg on-wheels · Nico · runners only" recurs where the book's block reads "Johannesburg & Greater Gauteng, on wheels"; "Kempton Park" appears only on the business-card back and the asphalt sheet.
- **Glows and dark tints** behind lockups and cards — off palette, as on the Templates (p12, p13).
- **Print sizes** — the business card canvas is 3.5 × 2 in with no bleed (South African cards are usually 90 × 55 mm + 3 mm bleed); the Runner ID fronts are drawn as mock-ups inside a frame, not trim-ready artwork.

## Questions for Andries Liebenberg

1. **"Runner" as a job title** — the badges read "RUNNER" / "FIELD RUNNER"; the book (p03) uses "runners" for the vehicles, and Nico is "owner and first driver".
2. **Chest-mark stitch** — the embroidery sheet adds an optional orange stitch under the mark; the book's polo spec (p11 "Embroidery — 70–90 mm wide on the chest") has none.
3. **Insurance and VAT notes** — the leave-behind back prints an insurance line (p24: no R3m / GIT / "insured" claims on any pricing asset) and "VAT TBD until locked" against p24 "Always say "excl. VAT"".
4. **Vehicle branding** — the bakkie target places a flag on the C-pillar; `sa-flag-edition.png` is the approved national mark (p10, p18 "SA flag: small accent only").
