# Motion

The hero loop, as the Brand Book specifies it (p21): "Hero loop: hero-locked-M.mp4 · 1280×720 · ~6.8 s · 60 fps · slow smooth camera drift · corner lockup stays sharp. Used on the website hero." Source file: Spoorlangs-hero-locked-M.mp4 (23 Sep 2026). The fetched site markup carries only the poster frame (`assets/Imagery/hero-locked-M-poster.png` as `.hero-image`, with no `<video>` element and `.hero-picture` a dead selector), so whether the live site plays the mp4 is not confirmed in the source pack — a gap. If it is played, wrap it to honour the site's reduced-motion rule, which collapses transitions but not animations or video: `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` should fall back to the poster.

Motion principles (p21): "Smooth, not flashy — slow drift and ease-out · UI transitions 200–400 ms; hero moves 4–8 s loops · Orange glow breathes gently; no strobing · Respect "reduced motion" settings". The site's own UI tempo is `.2s` on every hand-written transition with no easing set, hover lifts of `translateY(-2px)`, a `translateX(.35rem)` nudge on service cards, a 45° turn of the FAQ "+", and one `1s linear infinite` spin — at the floor of the book's range and without the ease-out (README §9 row 4, for Andries Liebenberg). Photography DO/DON'T apply to video as well: no "Racing as content, burnouts, track days", no "Cheap lens flares" (p19).

- `hero-locked-M.mp4` — the hero loop: night scene of the trusted driver and dark sedan on wet asphalt with ignition light trails, a slow camera drift, the lockup sharp in the corner; 1280×720, ~6.8 s, 60 fps, video/mp4, 12,775,295 B; no ground (full-bleed); orange light + black + chrome; Brand Book "Used on the website hero" (p21), locked look M (p19); loop it, muted, with `hero-locked-M-poster.png` as the poster and the `hero-shade-*` scrims over it as the site does over the still.
- `hero-locked-M-preview.gif` — a preview of the loop for thumbnails and documents where video cannot play; image/gif, 1,108,925 B; same scene; not used by the site. Do not use it as the hero itself — it has no 60 fps and no sound track, and a GIF cannot honour reduced-motion.
