# Templates — launch set and WhatsApp kit

Fifteen PNGs from the brand's own Canva designs (folders "Launch Editable Extra" and "WA Extra"): edit in Canva, re-export at the same size, replace the file. First exported 2026-10-08; on 2026-10-09 Andries Liebenberg corrected the designs in Canva and thirteen were re-exported — `edge-to-edge-delivery.png` and `instagram-launch-post.png` came back pixel-identical and are unchanged. Brand Book p27 lists "Website · WhatsApp kit · rate card · Email signature · Letterhead A4 · A5 leave-behind · Business card · Runner ID · Vehicle branding (target) · Workwear polo" as in use and "social master in Canva" as NOT BUILT YET — the squares are not that master. Its rule: "All templates follow this book: asphalt + ignition, real approved logos, Kempton Park city only, no fake stats." No file passes as exported yet. Colours were sampled from the PNGs with Pillow.

**Contact line (p25).** Every corrected piece prints `drive@spoorlangs.online` and the web address `spoorlangs.online`; no stored file prints any other domain or email address. Pieces that still carry an old address are not stored here.

## Do not reuse / fix first

- **Logo** — p13 "never: redraw, retype or AI-generate the wordmark"; p27 "real approved logos". Since 2026-10-09 the Wave-1 posts carry the approved lockup instead of the AI chrome/neon renders, but set on busy renders (p12 "on the darkest, calmest corner"; p13 "Don't place on busy texture"). Still not approved: `edge-to-edge` (the name typed in a geometric sans inside an orange plate), `instagram-launch-post` (a serif "Spoorlangs / Launch"), and a typed "SPOORLANGS" beside the real lockup on `wa-qr-status-story` (masthead) and `story-quote` (footer sign-off — recorded as unclear).
- **Fake stats** — p27 "no fake stats"; p28 "testimonials: none on file": `instagram-launch-post` five stars; `wave1-02` "8+ YEARS." unattributed (p03).
- **Imagery** — p19 DON'T "Racing as content, burnouts, track days", "Courier, trucking or car-transporter visuals"; brief "No stock or AI-generated images": `delivery-post`, `wave1-01/02/03/05`, `edge-to-edge` — concept until the real shoot.
- **Palette and contrast** — p14/p15: amber on `wave1-01/02/03` ("SOUTH AFRICA KEEPS MOVING" ≈#F8B030; "NOW DRIVING ACROSS GAUTENG" grades orange to amber — superseded #E8A317 family), #FE4D00 on `edge-to-edge`, navy ground on `wave1-02`; p26 "White on Ignition 2.6:1 Fail — avoid": `email-signature` (orange text on white), `edge-to-edge` body on the trail.
- **Pricing copy** — p24: "Local (<25 km)" and "miss written SLA" for "Local (under 25 km)" and "miss the agreed SLA".
- **Logo ground and clear space** — p11/p12: corrected 2026-10-09 — the black box is gone on `social-trust`, `social-service-cta`, `story-quote`, `wa-rate-card`; the `social-nico-factor` avatar is the flame-S on dark; the squares' lockups clear the kicker.
