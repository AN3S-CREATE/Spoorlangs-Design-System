# Imagery

The twelve photographs and composites the live site renders. All are concept imagery: the Brand Book's locked hero is a "Concept composite until the real shoot (approved Oct 2026) lands" (p19) and the licence record says "Photos: concept hero until the approved real shoot; log any stock licence" (p28). Treatment on the site: the hero `<img>` takes `object-fit: cover`, `filter: saturate(.96) contrast(1.08) brightness(.78)` under the two-layer `.hero-shade` scrim (`hero-shade-x-*`, `hero-shade-y-*`, one gradient `hero-shade-mobile-*` at ≤639px); the contact band sits under `.contact-overlay` (`contact-overlay-start/mid/end`); framed figures (`.wide-visual`, `.why-visual`, `.pod-layout figure`, `.service-detail-visual`) get `border: 1px solid var(--border); border-radius: .375rem; overflow: hidden`, the service spotlights at `aspect-ratio: 16/10` → `4/3` with an orange Oswald 3rem numeral bottom-right. Every image carries alt text (p26 "Alt text on every image and logo"); the hero is preloaded, the first spotlight `loading="eager"`, the rest `loading="lazy"`. Seven of the twelve are social tiles with copy baked in; that copy is artwork, not site copy — never quote it as Spoorlangs's words, and note the conflicts listed per file.

Photography direction (p19) — DO: "Handovers, drivers, collections, inspections · Real people, real vehicles, real roads · Wet asphalt, night or blue hour, restrained orange rim-light · Calm space for the logo and headline"; DON'T: "Racing as content, burnouts, track days · Stock "pointing at the car", dealership sales floors · Courier, trucking or car-transporter visuals · Cheap lens flares; AI text or fake logos on vehicles · A liveried fleet we don't run yet". On photography the logo is the Dark Mode lockup "on the darkest, calmest corner" (p12).

Shoot brief ("Spoorlangs - Photo Shoot Brief", real shoot approved October 2026), shot list in brief: 01 Handover at the client's door · 02 Nico, owner-driver with a real runner car, black polo · 03 Collection, confirming the job on the phone · 04 Inspection walk-around, checklist in hand · 05 Keys handover close-up, one orange accent · 06 POD on phone, client signs or confirms · 07 Gauteng roads at dusk, legal, steady · 08 Portraits for cut-outs on a plain dark backdrop · 09 Orange detail shots (left-chest mark, runner ID, key tag, light trails). DO: "Real people, real cars, real jobs · Calm, premium, professional · Dawn, dusk or soft overcast light · Asphalt blacks + one orange accent · Natural expressions, eyes on the job". DON'T: "No racing, speed, drifting or burnouts · No dealership stock lines or showrooms · No trucks, trailers or vans · No tow-only / non-runner scenes · No stock or AI-generated images". Specs: "≥ 3000 px long edge · RAW + high-quality JPEG · Landscape + portrait, every shot · Clean dark backgrounds for cut-outs; leave headline space"; wardrobe "Black polo with the left-chest Spoorlangs mark (white / titanium). Dark trousers, clean shoes. No other brands visible."; consent "Signed release from every person · Faces only with written consent · Blur plates & house numbers · Keep releases with the shoot files"; deliverables "graded JPEG + RAW + transparent PNG portrait cut-outs".
